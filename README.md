# VyaaparSahayak

AI-assisted business management and analytics platform for startups.

## Current Features

* **Product & Inventory Management** — Add products with pricing, categories, production details, and inventory; automatically update stock when orders are placed.

* **Order Management** — Record orders across sales channels with inventory validation and customer references.

* **Business Dashboard** — Track revenue, profit, orders, units sold, inventory, best/worst products, channel-wise performance, average order value, and inventory health.

* **Production Analytics** — Track production batches and calculate production cost per unit, production efficiency, cost leakage, and product margins.

* **Startup Profiles** — Store business type, industry, location, growth stage, MSME status, and annual revenue.

* **Government Scheme Matching** — Match startup profiles against stored government schemes based on revenue, business type, and MSME eligibility.

* **Business Risk Detection** — Identify channel dependency, dead stock, production cost leakage, and low-profit-margin risks and classify overall risk as Low, Medium, or High.

* **AI Action Planning** — Generate prioritized business recommendations based on detected risks through the OpenAI integration architecture.

## Tech Stack

**Python · FastAPI · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · Pydantic · Cohere API · REST APIs**
